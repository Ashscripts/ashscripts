The Scene

Wednesday afternoon. A new AshScript employee; Neha, a junior analyst; starts her first day. Her Autopilot laptop arrives preconfigured. She sits down, opens it, signs in with her work account. Kabir walks her through the setup:

"Open Outlook." Neha clicks Outlook. It opens. Her inbox is already there. Calendar already synced. Contacts already loaded. No setup wizard. No server names. No "enter your Exchange server" prompts. Neha looks up: "Is that it?"
"That's it," says Kabir. Meanwhile, across the room, Riya is onboarding a contractor the old way; before Intune. She's on the phone with helpdesk trying to explain what "Exchange ActiveSync server" means to someone who's never heard the term. Forty-five minutes later, the contractor's email still isn't working. Riya hangs up and walks over to Ashpak: "Email profiles. Everyone gets them. Now."

Why Settings Catalog for Windows Outlook instead of Email template? The Windows

email template configures the native Windows Mail app; not the full Outlook client. Most enterprises use the full Microsoft 365 Outlook app. The Settings Catalog gives access to hundreds of Outlook-specific settings that the simple Email template doesn't cover.
