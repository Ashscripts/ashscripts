Azure Monitor + Log Analytics Integration For advanced monitoring, Ashpak integrates Intune with Azure Monitor:

1 intune.microsoft.com 2 └── Tenant administration 3 → Diagnostic settings 4 → + Add diagnostic setting 5 6 Name: AshScript-Intune-Logs 7 8 LOG CATEGORIES TO COLLECT: 9 Audit logs 10 (every admin action in Intune) 11 Operational logs 12 (device enrollment, compliance changes) 13 Device compliance org logs 14 Devices and registration org logs 15 16 SEND TO: 17 Log Analytics workspace 18 Subscription: AshScript Azure 19 Workspace: AshScript-LogAnalytics 20

21 → Save

Sample Log Analytics queries:

1 // Alert: Multiple failed enrollments 2 // (possible attack or misconfiguration) 3 IntuneOperationalLogs 4 | where TimeGenerated > ago(24h) 5 | where OperationName == "Enrollment" 6 | where ResultType == "Failure" 7 | summarize FailureCount = count() by bin(TimeGenerated, 1h) 8 | where FailureCount > 10

1 // Track compliance state changes 2 // (who became non-compliant and when) 3 IntuneDeviceComplianceLogs 4 | where TimeGenerated > ago(7d) 5 | where NewComplianceState == "NonCompliant" 6 | project TimeGenerated, DeviceName, 7 UserName, OldState, NewComplianceState, 8 Reason 9 | order by TimeGenerated desc
