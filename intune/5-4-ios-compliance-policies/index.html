The Scene

Thursday afternoon. Ashpak moves to iOS. 200 corporate iPhones. ~300 BYOD enrolled iPhones. Two very different risk profiles. Two different compliance policies. Corporate phones are AshScript's property; maximum security requirements. BYOD phones belong to employees; stricter requirements would be intrusive and would drive users to unenroll.

"Corporate phones: enforce hard. BYOD phones: enforce reasonably. Both groups: block if jailbroken; non-negotiable."

iOS BYOD vs CORPORATE COMPARISON
SETTING CORPORATE BYOD
┼┼

Why no auto-retire on BYOD? Retiring a corporate device removes company data :

appropriate. Retiring a personal device via Intune removes the MDM profile and work data; also appropriate. But automatically retiring a personal phone after 60 days of non-compliance is too aggressive. A user's personal phone being offline for 60 days (holiday, switched phones) shouldn't trigger automatic removal. Ashpak removes the retire action from BYOD and handles it manually if needed.
