Lesson 6.4: iOS App Deployment: Apple VPP: Ashpak licences, purchases, and deploys apps to 200 iPhones at scale :

The Scene Tuesday afternoon. Android apps are deploying. Ashpak turns to iOS. 200 corporate iPhones. 300 BYOD enrolled iPhones. He pulls up the iOS app requirements:

Two challenges: Free apps are straightforward; but paid apps like DocuSign need volume licensing Pushing apps to iOS devices requires explicit user acceptance; unless IT uses Device Enrollment + Volume Purchase Program

Ashpak opens the Apple Business Manager portal.

Apple VPP; Volume Purchase Program VPP (now officially called Apple Business Manager content distribution) allows organisations to purchase app licences in bulk and deploy them silently; without requiring a personal Apple ID or user payment:

Step 1: Connect Apple Business Manager to Intune

1 ABM SETUP (Apple side): 2 business.apple.com

3 → Sign in as AshScript IT admin 4 → Settings → MDM Servers 5 → + Add MDM Server 6 Name: Microsoft Intune 7 → Upload MDM public key 8 (download from Intune portal) 9 → Save 10 → Download server token (.p7m file)

1 INTUNE SIDE: 2 intune.microsoft.com 3 └── Tenant Administration 4 → Connectors and Tokens 5 → Apple VPP Tokens 6 → + Add 7 Token name: AshScript-ABM-Token 8 Upload: [.p7m token file] 9 Country: India 10 Apple ID: intune-apple@ashscript.com 11 → Add 12 13 STATUS: Token active 14 Expires: [1 year from now] 15 (Must renew annually; calendar alert set)

Warning: VPP token expiry is critical. The Apple VPP token expires annually. If it expires, Intune loses the ability to assign and revoke app licences. Set a calendar reminder 60 days before expiry. Renewal takes 10 minutes but forgetting it causes app deployment failures fleet-wide.

Where did these licences come from? Ashpak (or the procurement team) purchased them in Apple Business Manager before connecting to Intune. Free apps (Outlook, Teams, Authenticator) have unlimited free licences available through ABM. Paid apps (DocuSign, Lightroom) require purchasing the required number of licences in ABM first.

iOS App Licence Monitoring

Licence alerts: Set a recurring check of the licence report. When utilisation hits 90%, purchase more. Running out of licences causes app deployments to silently fail; users don't get the app and IT doesn't always get a clear notification.

Troubleshooting iOS App Deployment

Issue 1: VPP App Shows "Not Installed" Despite Assignment

1 SYMPTOM: App assigned as Required to corporate 2 iPhones but shows "Not installed" 3 4 COMMON CAUSES: 5 × Device not supervised 6 (Prevent removal + device licensing 7 require supervised enrollment)

8 × VPP token expired 9 × Insufficient licences 10 11 DIAGNOSIS: 12 intune.microsoft.com → Devices → [iPhone] 13 → Hardware → Supervised: Yes/No? 14 If No: Device isn't supervised 15 (ADE enrollment required) 16 17 Apps → iOS VPP apps 18 → Token status: Active? 19 → Available licences > 0? 20 21 FIX: 22 Supervise devices via ADE (Apple DEP) 23 (re-enrollment may be required for 24 existing devices) 25 Renew VPP token if expired 26 Purchase more licences if exhausted

Issue 2: User Gets "This app requires payment" Prompt

1 SYMPTOM: User gets App Store payment prompt 2 for a VPP app that should be free 3 via IT 4 5 CAUSE: App assigned with User licensing 6 but user has no Managed Apple ID 7 OR: Licence type mismatch 8 9 FIX: 10 Check assignment licence type: 11 Apps → [app] → Properties 12 License type: Device vs User? 13 14 For supervised corporate iPhones: 15 Switch to Device licensing 16 (eliminates Apple ID requirement 17 entirely) 18 19 For BYOD: 20 User needs Managed Apple ID 21 (created in Apple Business Manager 22 and invited to user's device)

Issue 3: LOB App (.ipa) Stops Installing After Certificate Expiry

1 SYMPTOM: Custom CRM .ipa app stops installing 2 on new iPhones; existing installs 3 still work 4 5 CAUSE: Apple Distribution certificate used 6 to sign the .ipa has expired 7 (certificates valid 1 year) 8 9 DIAGNOSIS: 10 In Intune: Apps → AshScript CRM (.ipa) 11 → Properties: Certificate expiry date? 12 13 FIX: 14 1. Renew Apple Distribution certificate 15 (developer.apple.com) 16 2. Re-sign the .ipa with new certificate 17 3. Upload new .ipa version to Intune 18 4. Intune pushes update to devices 19 20 PREVENTION: 21 Set calendar alert 60 days before cert 22 expiry; same as VPP token 23 OR: Migrate to Custom App via ABM 24 (no annual .ipa certificate concern)
