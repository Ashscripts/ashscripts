The Scene

Thursday evening. The iOS policies are live. Ashpak turns to Android; the most complex platform for compliance because of the four enrollment modes: Each mode needs a different compliance policy. The kiosk scanners are shared devices with no user account; traditional compliance doesn't apply. The BYOD work profile only evaluates the work profile side. The corporate phones get the most rigorous checks.
