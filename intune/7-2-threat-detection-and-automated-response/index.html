Lesson 7.2; Threat Detection and Automated Response; Ashpak configures MDE to detect, investigate, and respond automatically ;

The Scene Tuesday morning. 831 devices are onboarded. The MDE dashboard shows 2 high-severity alerts Ashpak hasn't investigated yet. He opens them: Alert 1:

1 HIGH: Ransomware-linked behaviour detected 2 Device: ASHPC-SALES-19 3 User: sales19@ashscript.com 4 Time: Yesterday 23:41 5 Detail: Rapid file encryption pattern detected 6 in Documents folder. 847 files renamed 7 with .crypted extension in 4 minutes.

Alert 2:

1 HIGH: Credential dumping attempt 2 Device: ASHPC-DEV-07 3 User: dev07@ashscript.com 4 Time: Today 08:12 5 Detail: Process attempted to read LSASS memory. 6 Process: suspicious_tool.exe 7 Blocked by Credential Guard

Two high-severity threats. On real employee devices. Right now. Ashpak takes a breath. He was going to spend today configuring automated response policies. Instead, he's going to do both; investigate these threats AND configure automation so future threats handle themselves.

Alert 1: Ransomware Response Immediate Actions:

1 security.microsoft.com 2 └── Incidents & alerts → Alerts 3 → HIGH: Ransomware-linked behaviour 4 → ASHPC-SALES-19 5 6 STEP 1: Isolate immediately 7 Device actions → Isolate device 8 (Stops any potential spread to network) 9 10 STEP 2: Review alert details 11 Files encrypted: 847 files in Documents 12 Process responsible: explorer.exe 13 (unusual; Explorer shouldn't mass-rename) 14 └── Child: crypted_installer.exe 15 └── Launched by: user double-clicked 16 From: Downloads folder 17 File: Invoice_Dec2024.exe 18 [FAKE INVOICE; MALWARE] 19 20 STEP 3: Check for lateral movement 21 Advanced Hunting query: 22 DeviceNetworkEvents 23 | where DeviceName == "ASHPC-SALES-19" 24 | where Timestamp > ago(24h) 25 | where RemotePort in (445, 139, 3389) 26 (SMB/RDP connections; lateral movement 27 indicators) 28 29 Result: 0 events 30 (No lateral movement detected) 31 32 STEP 4: Check other devices 33 Did anyone else get the same email? 34 Advanced Hunting: 35 EmailEvents 36 | where Subject contains "Invoice_Dec" 37 | where Timestamp > ago(24h) 38 39 Result: 3 other recipients ← warn them

Configuring Automated Investigation and Response (AIR) Ashpak configures MDE to automatically investigate and remediate threats without waiting for manual approval:

1 security.microsoft.com 2 └── Settings → Endpoints 3 └── Advanced features 4 → Automated investigation: On 5 → Automatically resolve alerts: On 6 7 └── Auto remediation level 8 Device groups: All devices 9 Automation level: 10 ● Full; remediate threats 11 automatically 12 (Trusted; MDE investigates 13 AND remediates without 14 manual approval)

"Block executable files unless they meet prevalence/age criteria" is one of the most powerful ASR rules. When enabled, any executable that: Has been downloaded from the internet recently (< 24 hours) Is rarely seen across Microsoft's global telemetry Is not digitally signed by a trusted publisher

...is blocked from running. The Invoice_Dec2024.exe that triggered Alert 1 would have been blocked by this rule before it ever executed.

Configuring Alert Notifications Ashpak needs to be notified immediately when high-severity alerts occur; not discover them the morning after:

1 security.microsoft.com 2 └── Settings → Microsoft Defender XDR 3 └── Email notifications 4 → + Add notification rule 5 6 Name: AshScript-HighSeverity-Alerts 7 8 Alert severity: 9 High 10 Informational (for specific alert types) 11 12 Alert categories: 13 Ransomware 14 Credential theft 15 Network anomaly 16 Suspicious behaviour 17 18 Notification recipients: 19 ashpak.it@ashscript.com 20 security@ashscript.com 21 22 → Save

Custom Detection Rules Ashpak creates custom detection rules that trigger alerts based on specific behaviour patterns at AshScript:

1 security.microsoft.com 2 └── Hunting → Custom detections 3 → + Create detection rule 4 5 Name: AshScript-LargeEmailExfil-Detection 6 Description: Alert when user forwards 10+ emails 7 to external address within 1 hour 8 (insider threat / account compromise)

KQL Query:

1 EmailEvents 2 | where Timestamp > ago(1h) 3 | where DeliveryAction == "Delivered" 4 | where RecipientEmailAddress !endswith "@ashscript.com" 5 | where EmailDirection == "Outbound" 6 | summarize 7 EmailCount = count(), 8 Recipients = make_set(RecipientEmailAddress) 9 by SenderFromAddress, bin(Timestamp, 1h) 10 | where EmailCount >= 10

11 | project 12 Timestamp, 13 SenderFromAddress, 14 EmailCount, 15 Recipients

1 Alert settings: 2 Severity: High 3 Category: Exfiltration 4 Run frequency: Every 1 hour 5 Alert title: Potential mass email exfiltration 6 7 → Save

Custom detections run as KQL queries on a schedule. They extend MDE's built-in detections with organisation-specific logic. Ashpak builds several: Mass file access in SharePoint (> 500 files in 10 min) Login from country not in approved travel list USB device connected to Finance/Legal laptops PowerShell with encoded commands executed New local admin account created
