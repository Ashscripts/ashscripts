Lesson 8.5: The Weekly Operations Checklist: Ashpak's recurring rhythm that keeps AshScript's fleet healthy forever :

The Scene Thursday morning. Riya's board meeting is today at 2 PM. Ashpak walks in at 9 AM and opens his laptop. He doesn't search for reports. He doesn't scramble. He has a checklist; the same checklist he runs every Thursday morning, every week. It takes 45 minutes. It tells him everything he needs to know about the state of AshScript's devices.
