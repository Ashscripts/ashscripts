The Scene

Tuesday morning. Ashpak arrives to find two sticky notes on his monitor: Note 1 (from Priya):

"Every morning I have to manually type the corporate Wi-Fi password on my MacBook. Also the password changed last week and nobody told me; I was offline for 2 hours. Please fix."

Note 2 (from Sales Director):

"3 sales reps working from client sites can't connect to the CRM. They need VPN. Setting up VPN manually is too complicated; one rep accidentally connected to the wrong server. We need this automated."

Ashpak reads both notes and nods. These are exactly the problems configuration profiles are designed to eliminate.

"After today," he thinks, "no AshScript employee will ever manually configure Wi-Fi or VPN again. And when the Wi-Fi password changes, every device updates automatically."

He opens the Intune portal.

Part 2: VPN Profiles

A VPN profile pushes the corporate VPN configuration to devices. Users don't need to install or configure the VPN client; it's pre-configured and ready to connect. Lockdown mode: If the VPN connection drops on a Finance laptop, all network traffic is

blocked until VPN reconnects. No data can leave the device over an unprotected connection. This is the highest security option; appropriate for Finance but too restrictive for general employees.
