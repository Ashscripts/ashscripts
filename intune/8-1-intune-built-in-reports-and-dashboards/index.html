The Scene

The Scene Monday morning. Phase 8. The last phase. Ashpak arrives to find three messages waiting: From Riya (CEO):

"Board meeting Thursday. I need a one-page summary: how many devices are managed, how many are compliant, what threats we've had, what apps are deployed. Can you give me numbers?"

From Kabir (IT Manager):

"Two users called this morning; their laptops show as non-compliant but they don't know why. Can we get a report that shows non-compliant devices AND the specific reason for each one?"

From the Finance team:

"Our DocuSign licences; we think we're running low. Can you check how many we've used?"

Three completely different reporting needs. One morning. Ashpak opens the Intune portal.

"This is what Phase 8 is about. The devices are managed. The security is in place. Now we need to see it all clearly; and prove it."

Device Health Report: Answering Riya To answer Riya's board request, Ashpak pulls the comprehensive device health report:

1 intune.microsoft.com 2 └── Reports → Device Management 3 → All devices 4 5 FULL EXPORT (CSV): 6 847 rows; one per device

Why total users (1331) exceeds devices (847)? Compliance is measured per user-device pair. A user with 2 devices (laptop + phone) has 2 compliance evaluations. The compliance count reflects unique user-device pairs, not just physical devices.
