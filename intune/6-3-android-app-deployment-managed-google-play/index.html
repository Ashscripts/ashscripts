The Scene

The Scene Tuesday morning. Ashpak opens the Android device list. 250 corporate Samsung phones. The consumer Google Play Store is blocked (from Lesson 4.6 restriction profiles). Which means right now, users can't install anything at all. His phone buzzes. It's a message from the Sales Director:

"Ashpak; my team's phones don't have Outlook. They can't check email. They can't open the CRM. Half the team is using their personal phones to check work email. This is unacceptable."

Ashpak checks the enrollment status. The phones are enrolled. Configuration profiles applied. Compliance: passing. But zero apps deployed. He looks at the 20 warehouse scanners. Zero apps. The inventory app isn't installed. Warehouse staff can't scan anything. He looks at the 30 COPE executive phones. No Outlook. No Teams. 300 enrolled BYOD Android devices. Work profile exists but no work apps in it.

"Every Android device. Zero apps. Today we fix that."

He navigates to the Managed Google Play setup in Intune.

Step 1; Connect Intune to Managed Google Play Before deploying any Android apps, Ashpak must link Intune to Managed Google Play:

Enterprise binding is permanent. Once an enterprise account is bound to Intune, all enrolled Android Enterprise devices are associated with it. If you accidentally bind to the wrong Google account, you need to factory reset all affected devices to rebind. Always use a dedicated IT service account; never a personal Google account.

Step 2; Approve Apps in Managed Google Play Before Intune can deploy an Android app, it must be approved in the Managed Google Play store. This is the IT vetting step:

Step 4; Deploying a Private/Custom App AshScript has a custom CRM app built in-house. It's not on the public Play Store. Ashpak uploads it as a private Managed Google Play app:

Private apps vs public apps: Public apps (Outlook, Teams) are approved from the public catalogue; Google hosts them, updates are handled by Google. Private apps are uploaded by IT; IT controls the version, updates require a new APK upload, and the app is invisible to anyone outside AshScript's managed Play store.

Troubleshooting Android App Deployment

Issue 1: App Shows "Pending" for Days

1 SYMPTOM: App assignment shows "Pending" 2 on Android device for 24+ hours 3 4 COMMON CAUSES: 5 × Device not syncing with Intune 6 × Managed Google Play not connected 7 (new tenant; binding not complete) 8 × App not approved in MGP 9 (most common mistake) 10 × Device Wi-Fi issue 11 12 DIAGNOSIS: 13 On device: Company Portal → Sync 14 Check approval: 15 intune.microsoft.com → Apps → [app] 16 Is it showing as "Managed Google Play 17 app"? Or "Not approved"? 18 19 FIX: 20 Re-approve app in MGP store: 21 intune.microsoft.com → Apps → + Add 22 → Managed Google Play app 23 → Search and approve the app again 24 Force device sync

Issue 2: Private APK Install Fails

1 SYMPTOM: Private AshScript CRM app fails 2 to install on devices 3 Error: "Installation failed" 4 5 COMMON CAUSES: 6 × APK targets too-old Android API level 7 (Google requires minimum API level 30 8 for new apps) 9 × APK signing certificate mismatch 10 (updating app with different signing key 11 than previous version) 12 × APK too large; upload timeout 13 14 DIAGNOSIS: 15 Device event log: 16 Settings → Apps → CRM → App details 17 → Check error message on device 18 19 In Intune:

20 Apps → AshScript CRM 21 → Device install status → Failed devices 22 → Error code shown 23 24 FIX (API level): 25 Rebuild APK targeting API 30+ 26 Coordinate with dev team 27 28 FIX (signing): 29 Use same signing certificate as original 30 If lost: new package name needed 31 (users get new install not update)

Issue 3: Work Profile App Not Appearing

1 SYMPTOM: BYOD user says Outlook not appearing 2 in work profile Play Store 3 Despite being assigned as Available 4 5 CAUSE: Work profile not fully set up 6 OR app assigned to wrong group 7 8 DIAGNOSIS: 9 Check device group membership: 10 intune.microsoft.com → Devices 11 → [device] → Group membership 12 Is INTUNE-DEV-ANDROID-BYOD listed? 13 14 Check user vs device assignment: 15 Apps → Outlook → Assignments 16 Is Available assigned to correct 17 group type? 18 (User group vs device group matters 19 for work profile devices) 20 21 FIX: 22 Ensure assignment uses USER group 23 for work profile devices: 24 Available: INTUNE-USR-ALL 25 (User-based assignment ensures work 26 profile receives the app) 27 Force device sync
