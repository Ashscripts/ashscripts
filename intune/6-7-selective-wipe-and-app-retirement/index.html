Lesson 6.7: Selective Wipe and App Retirement: Ashpak removes company data surgically without touching personal content :

The Scene Friday morning. Ashpak has just finished the APP policies. His phone rings. It's Kabir:

"Three situations. One; Priya from Design lost her personal iPhone on the Mumbai local train. She wants IT to remove her work emails and Teams messages before anyone finds the phone. Two; a contractor finished his project yesterday, his access was removed, but he still has Outlook and Teams with company data on his personal Android. Three; we're decommissioning the old AshScript CRM and moving to a new one. 250 phones have the old CRM installed. How do we remove it everywhere?"

Three different scenarios. Three different tools:

Scenario 1; Selective Wipe: Priya's Lost iPhone Priya's iPhone is enrolled in MDM (BYOD enrolled). She hasn't lost a corporate device; it's her personal phone. A full wipe would destroy her personal photos, contacts, and data. Ashpak uses selective wipe (retire):

7 8 RETIRE ACTION: 9 Removes: MDM profiles 10 Corporate Wi-Fi config 11 Corporate VPN config 12 Corporate email profile 13 App Protection Policy data 14 All company data from managed apps 15 16 Keeps: Personal photos 17 Personal apps 18 Personal contacts 19 Personal messages 20 iCloud data 21 22 Result: Phone looks like it was never 23 enrolled; company completely removed

Warning: Retire is not instant. The retire command is sent to the device the next time it checks in with Intune. If the phone is offline or the thief has it in airplane mode, the retire command waits. For immediate data protection on a lost device with an APP policy, the company data is already protected; the thief can't access Outlook without the PIN, and if they factory reset the phone, company data is wiped anyway.

Scenario 2; App Selective Wipe: Contractor's Unenrolled Android The contractor's personal Android was never enrolled in MDM; he only used Outlook and Teams with an APP policy (MAM-only). Ashpak can't use device actions (no MDM). He uses App selective wipe:

15 16 WIPE DIALOG: 17 User: contractor@ashscript.com 18 Devices: Samsung Galaxy S23 (unmanaged) 19 → Wipe 20 21 RESULT: 22 Next time Outlook or Teams opens: 23 All company email: deleted 24 All Teams messages: deleted 25 All OneDrive files: removed 26 All company app data: gone 27 Personal apps: untouched 28 Personal photos/contacts: untouched

Scenario 3; App Retirement: Remove Old CRM from 250 Phones The old AshScript CRM needs to be removed from all 250 corporate Android phones. This is handled via an Uninstall assignment:

1 intune.microsoft.com 2 └── Apps → All apps 3 └── AshScript CRM v1 (old version) 4 → Properties → Assignments 5 → + Add group 6 7 Assignment type: Uninstall 8 Group: INTUNE-DEV-ANDROID-CORP 9 → OK → Save 10 11 RESULT: 12 Next time each of the 250 phones checks in: 13 AshScript CRM v1: uninstalled 14 All CRM app data: removed 15 Other apps: untouched 16 User data outside CRM: untouched 17 18 TIMELINE: 19 Devices check in every 8 hours 20 All 250 phones: CRM removed within 24 hours

Uninstall vs Retire vs Wipe:

1 Use UNINSTALL assignment when: 2 You want to remove a SPECIFIC APP 3 from ENROLLED devices 4 Other apps and device data stay

Use RETIRE when: You want to remove ALL company data and profiles from a BYOD enrolled device Personal data stays

Use FULL WIPE when: You want to factory reset a device Corporate device; total wipe Lost corporate device with sensitive data

Issue 1: Retire Command Pending for Days

1 SYMPTOM: Retire sent to device 3 days ago 2 Status: Pending 3 Device not retired 4 5 CAUSE: Device offline / no network 6 Stolen device in airplane mode 7 Device powered off 8 9 WHAT HAPPENS: 10 Command waits in Intune queue 11 Executes when device next connects 12 13 FOR LOST DEVICES; IMMEDIATE PROTECTION: 14 APP Policy already protects data: 15 → Cannot open Outlook without PIN 16 → Data encrypted at rest 17 → Cannot copy data to personal apps 18 19 Physical theft of encrypted device: 20 → Thief cannot read data without PIN 21 22 ESCALATION: 23 If device contains highly sensitive data 24 and has been missing 7+ days: 25 Report to legal/security team 26 Consider issuing police report 27 (IMEI number from Intune → police)

Key Concepts to Lock In: Lessons 6.6 + 6.7

Concept Remember This

APP vs MDM APP = app-level data protection; MDM = device management

APP without enrollment Works on unenrolled personal devices; no MDM needed

Send org data to Policy managed apps = data stays in work apps only

Conditional launch: disabled account Auto-wipes company data when account is disabled in Entra

Selective wipe (retire) Removes MDM + company data from enrolled BYOD; personal safe

App selective wipe (MAM) Works on unenrolled devices: company data only

Full wipe Factory reset; corporate devices only

Concept Remember This

Uninstall assignment Removes specific app from enrolled devices

Wipe is not instant Device must check in; pending state until online

Revoke sessions Forces re-auth → triggers APP wipe on stubbornly offline device

Screenshot blocked iOS: notification blocking; Android: screen capture + Assistant

Open-in filtering Managed apps only in share sheet; supervised devices only
