The Scene

Monday morning. Ashpak's phone rings at 8:52 AM. It's Kabir:

"Ashpak, I've got three issues from the Sales team this morning. First; one rep accidentally added his personal Google account to his work phone and now his personal photos are syncing everywhere. Second; someone changed the Wi-Fi settings on their phone and now it can't connect to the office network. Third; the Sales Director wants to know why his reps are on Instagram during work hours when they're supposed to be client-facing."

Ashpak opens a notepad and writes three lines:

"All three are configuration profile problems," he says. "Give me today." He opens Intune and navigates to Android Enterprise configuration. 250 corporate Android phones. 20 kiosk scanners. 300 BYOD work profiles. Three completely different management needs. He starts with the corporate phones.

OEMConfig explained: Standard Android Enterprise MDM policies are defined by Google

and apply to all Android devices equally. OEMConfig is a vendor extension; each manufacturer (Samsung, Zebra, Honeywell) publishes an OEMConfig app that exposes their device-specific management capabilities through a standardised interface. Samsung's Knox Service Plugin unlocks hundreds of additional Samsung-specific settings.
