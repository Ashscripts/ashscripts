Lesson 6.2: Deploying Microsoft 365 Apps: Ashpak pushes Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams to 800 laptops silently :

The Scene Monday late morning. The app audit spreadsheet is still on Ashpak's screen. He filters to the most critical gap:

127 Windows laptops don't have the full Microsoft 365 suite. Users are working with older Office versions, some even Office 2016. 31 MacBooks are in the same state. 203 more devices have M365 but haven't updated in months; security vulnerabilities, feature gaps, compatibility issues. Riya's note from the audit: "We pay for M365 E3 licences for everyone. Every laptop should have the apps. Why don't they?" Because nobody deployed them centrally. Each employee either had Office pre-installed from the manufacturer, downloaded it themselves, or went without. No standardisation. No version control. No central management. Ashpak is about to change that.

Microsoft AutoUpdate (MAU) on macOS: MAU is the background update service for Microsoft apps on Mac. When configured via Intune, IT controls the update channel and timing; users don't need to manually update Word, Excel, or Outlook. Updates happen silently in the background.

Troubleshooting M365 App Deployment

Issue 1: M365 Fails with Error 0x87D1041C

1 SYMPTOM: M365 deployment fails 2 Error: 0x87D1041C 3

4 MEANING: App not detected after installation 5 attempt; or install failed silently 6 7 COMMON CAUSES: 8 × Old MSI Office not fully removed 9 (Remove MSI versions: Yes; but removal 10 sometimes fails on stubborn installs) 11 × Insufficient disk space 12 (M365 Apps requires ~10GB free) 13 × Pending Windows restart blocking install 14 15 DIAGNOSIS: 16 On device: C:\Program Files (x86)\Microsoft 17 Office\root\; is old Office here? 18 Check disk space: This PC → C: drive free? 19 Check Event Viewer: 20 Application Log → Source: MsiInstaller 21 Look for Office-related errors 22 23 FIX: 24 Manually remove old Office: 25 support. microsoft.com/office-removal-tool 26 Run Microsoft's Support and Recovery 27 Assistant (SaRA) tool 28 Free disk space if needed 29 Restart device → force Intune sync

Issue 2: Teams Keeps Updating to Wrong Version

1 SYMPTOM: Teams installs correct version then 2 updates itself to a different version 3 against IT policy 4 5 CAUSE: Teams has its own auto-update 6 mechanism separate from M365 channel 7 8 FIX: 9 Use Teams Update Policy in Teams Admin Center: 10 admin.teams. microsoft.com 11 → Update policies 12 → Create policy 13 Update channel: Monthly Enterprise 14 Assign to affected users 15 16 OR: Use Intune Settings Catalog 17 Search "Teams" → Update channel settings

Issue 3: M365 Apps Installed but Wrong Language

1 SYMPTOM: M365 installs in English but user 2 needs Hindi/regional language 3 4 CAUSE: Language setting not configured 5 or OS language mismatch 6 7 FIX: 8 Edit M365 deployment profile: 9 Languages section: 10 Add: Hindi (hi-IN) explicitly 11 + Match OS language: Yes 12 13 Alternatively: Deploy language pack 14 via separate Win32 app deployment 15 (Advanced language control for specific 16 user groups)
