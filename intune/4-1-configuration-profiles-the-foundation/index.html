The Scene

Monday morning. Phase 3 is complete. 847 devices are enrolled in Intune. Ashpak opens the portal and stares at the device list with quiet satisfaction; then Kabir walks in:

"Ashpak, Priya just called. Her new MacBook enrolled fine but there's no Wi-Fi profile; she can't connect to the corporate network automatically. She has to type the WPA2 password every time."

Before Ashpak can respond, his phone buzzes. It's a message from the Sales Director:

"My reps enrolled their phones but the VPN isn't configured. They can't reach the CRM from outside the office."

Then an email from IT Security:

"Enrolled Windows laptops are missing the certificate for internal app authentication. Users are getting certificate errors."

Ashpak holds up a hand.

"Stop. All of these are the same problem. We enrolled 847 devices and pushed zero configuration. An enrolled device with no configuration is just a managed blank slate. We need configuration profiles."

He opens a new browser tab and navigates to: Phase 4 begins.

What Are Configuration Profiles?

intune.microsoft.com

Configuration

A configuration profile is a package of settings that Intune pushes to a device; silently, automatically, without user involvement. Think of it as a cloud-delivered replacement for Group Policy (on Windows) or a replacement for manual device setup (on macOS, iOS, Android).
