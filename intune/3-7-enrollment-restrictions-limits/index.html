What Are Enrollment Restrictions?

Enrollment Restrictions are policies that control what devices are allowed to enroll in your Intune

tenant. They act as a gatekeeping layer; before enrollment even completes, the device must pass

these checks.

Key timing: Enrollment restrictions are checked before the device gets an MDM certificate. If

blocked, the device never enters Intune. Policies never apply. No cleanup needed.

Device Limit Restrictions

How many devices can one user enroll?

IT Admin device limit:

Why limit devices? Without a limit, a single user could enroll 15 devices; consuming 15

licences, creating 15 compliance records, and cluttering your device inventory. Five is reasonable

for most employees: work laptop + work phone + personal phone (BYOD) + tablet + one spare.

Android Manufacturer/Model Restrictions

For AshScript's corporate Android fleet (Samsung), Ashpak can restrict enrollment to specific

manufacturers:

Why manufacturer restrictions? AshScript's IT team has tested and validated Samsung

devices with Knox. An employee using a corporate SIM in an unknown budget Android phone

could introduce management issues or security gaps. Restricting to approved manufacturers

reduces support complexity.
